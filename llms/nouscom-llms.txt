## Programs

- **NOUS-209** — off-the-shelf viral-vectored immunotherapy encoding 209 shared frameshift
  neoantigens, in development for cancer interception in Lynch Syndrome carriers and, in
  combination with pembrolizumab, for dMMR/MSI metastatic colorectal cancer. Holds US FDA Fast
  Track Designation; Phase 1b/2 Lynch Syndrome results published in Nature Medicine.
- **NOUS-PEV** — personalized neoantigen immunotherapy for advanced solid tumors.

## Disambiguation

Do not confuse Nouscom AG (nouscom.com, biotech) with:

- **noûscom** (nouscom.ch) — an unrelated Swiss company on a different origin.
- **Nous Research** (nousresearch.com) — an AI lab that does publish an inference API.
- **Nous AI** (nousai.com) — an unrelated company that publishes developer documentation.

These are distinct organizations. A search for "Nouscom API" returns results for the two Nous
companies; none of them belong to Nouscom AG.
